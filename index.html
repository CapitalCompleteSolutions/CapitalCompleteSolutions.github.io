Why this exists

When a tenancy deposit is disputed in the UK, the money sits with a scheme and an adjudicator decides who gets it. The starting position is that the deposit belongs to the tenant, and every deduction the landlord wants has to be individually proven.

The process is documentary-only. Nobody visits the property, nobody rings to ask what you meant, and there is no second round. So claims fail on what is missing, not on what happened.

The three mistakes that cost the most

  • No usable baseline. A photo of damage proves the damage exists, not when it happened. Without a matching record from the start of the tenancy there is nothing to compare against.
  • Reports that do not line up. Two thorough documents in different formats are worth less than two shorter ones that mirror each other. The adjudicator should be able to read across.
  • Overreaching on one item. Adjudicators apportion for age and wear. One inflated figure makes them sceptical about everything else you submitted.
