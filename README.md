# Deposit Evidence Checklist

A free, offline checklist for UK landlords and letting agents preparing a tenancy deposit claim.

**Live:** https://capitalcompletesolutions.github.io/

## What it is

When a tenancy deposit is disputed in the UK, the deposit is treated as the tenant's money until
the landlord proves otherwise. Adjudication is documentary-only — nobody visits the property and
there is no second round. Claims fail on what is missing, not on what happened.

This is the checklist we work through ourselves before a claim goes in, covering four stages:

- **The baseline** — does a usable check-in record actually exist?
- **The check-out** — does it mirror the check-in closely enough to compare?
- **The claim** — is each deduction evidenced, measured and proportionate?
- **The bundle** — is it ordered so an adjudicator can follow it in one pass?

Tick items off, then copy the whole thing out as plain text for your case file.

## Privacy

There is no backend, no accounts and no analytics. Your progress is stored in your own browser's
`localStorage` and never leaves the page. The whole thing is one static HTML file.
